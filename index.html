Plate

Text it what's on your mind. It handles the rest.

Plate is a text message assistant. It keeps your list and uses your Google Calendar to know when you are free.

Join Plate

What it does

  1. You text Plate the things you need to get done, in plain words.
  2. Plate keeps your list and texts you one next thing to do, at a time that fits your day.
  3. You reply in plain words, like "done", "push it", or "I have 30 minutes free". Plate reshuffles your day.
  4. Optional: connect your Google Calendar. Plate reads your events so it knows when you are busy. It adds an event only when you ask, like "put gym on my calendar tomorrow at 5". Text "undo" and it takes that event off again.

Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026.

How Plate texts you

  • Plate sends and gets texts over iMessage. A service called Blooio carries them.
  • You need a phone that can use iMessage. If a text ever goes out another way, Plate stops texting you until you text first.

What Plate stores

  • Your phone number.
  • What you tell Plate when you sign up. That is your name and your time zone. It is also when you wake up and go to sleep, your class or work hours, your meal times, what matters most to you, and any notes you add.
  • Your tasks: their names, how long they take, when they are due, and whether they are done.
  • Your last few messages and Plate's replies, up to four, for up to 2 hours. Then Plate erases them. Plate keeps them only so it can follow what you said a moment ago. A calendar request you have not finished is kept for up to 30 minutes, then erased.
  • Counts and settings, like how many texts Plate sent today, whether you paused it, and when you last texted.
  • If you connect your calendar, Plate stores a sign-in code from Google (encrypted) and the date you connected. It stores the titles and times of your events for the next 7 days. It also stores the name, time and ID of the last event it added, so it can undo it. It does not store all-day events.
  • If you use the Join Plate form on this page: your phone number, the date and time, the page address, and the words you agreed to.
  • If you text Plate without an invite: your phone number, the date you first texted, and how many times you texted. Plate answers once and then stays quiet.
  • Short technical records of message IDs (no message text), so Plate never answers the same text twice.
  • Plate keeps all of this until you delete it. The only exception is message text, which is erased after 2 hours.
  • Photos and screenshots: Plate does not open or save them. It ignores the picture. It still reads any words you send with it.

Who else sees your information

  • An AI service (Anthropic). Plate reads simple replies like "done" or "push" on its own. For anything else, it sends your message to Anthropic so an AI can write the reply. It also sends your tasks, your sign-up answers (not your phone number), your last few messages, and the titles and times of your coming calendar events. Plate sends this only to get your reply written. Anthropic's privacy policy covers what it receives.
  • Blooio. It carries every text, so it sees them and keeps its own records. Plate cannot erase those.
  • Supabase. It hosts Plate's database and program. Your stored information lives there.
  • Google. Only if you connect your calendar. See the next section.
  • Server logs. Plate's server keeps short technical notes to fix bugs. They can show the last four digits of a phone number and error messages. When an AI answer goes wrong, a note can hold a short piece of it.
  • The person who runs Plate can see your information only to fix problems you report.
  • Plate does not sell your information. It does not share it for marketing or ads.

Your Google Calendar (only if you connect it)

  • Plate asks Google for two permissions: calendar.readonly and calendar.events.
  • Plate reads the titles and times of your events. It uses them to know when you are busy and to check for clashes before it adds something.
  • Plate adds an event only when you ask. The event gets the title you typed and a reminder 10 minutes before. Plate marks it as its own.
  • Plate removes only events it added itself. Text "undo" and it takes off the last one it added. It checks its mark first. It does not change or remove your other events.
  • Google's calendar.events permission is broad. It would let an app edit any event. Plate does not do that. It only adds events you ask for and removes events it added.
  • Event titles and times also go to the AI service above, so Plate can plan around them.
  • Text "disconnect calendar" to stop. Plate tells Google to cancel its access, erases your sign-in code, and erases the calendar events it stored. You can also remove Plate in your Google Account settings.
  • Plate does not sell Google data. It does not use it for ads. It does not use it to train AI models. It uses it only to run Plate.
  • Plate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google calendar data is used only to provide the scheduling feature you see in Plate, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Stop and delete

  • Text STOP. Plate stops texting you and erases the messages it kept. It keeps your other information until you delete it. Text START to turn texts back on.
  • Text DELETE. Plate asks you to confirm. Text YES DELETE within 10 minutes. Then Plate erases your profile, your tasks, your calendar connection (and tells Google to cancel its access), your Join form record, and every message it kept. It keeps your invite, so you can start over if you want, and the short message-ID records (no text).
  • Plate cannot erase records that Blooio, Anthropic, Google or Supabase keep on their side. Ask them.
  • You can also email the address below and ask for your information to be erased.

Text message terms

  • Plate is invite-only right now. It texts you only after you text it first, and only if you are invited. If you are not invited, it answers once and then stays quiet.
  • The Join Plate form on this page saves your phone number and the words you agreed to, as proof that you agreed.
  • Plate texts you in two ways. It answers your messages, and it sends you your next task. For some requests, like adding a calendar event, it sends two texts: a short "on it", then the answer.
  • How often: Plate waits at least 90 minutes between texts it starts. It stays quiet while you sleep, using the times you gave it. It starts at most 8 texts a day. On Sundays it texts only about things that are due soon or very important. If you have not answered its last three texts, it waits until you text. After 3 days without a text from you, it stops until you text again.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. Reply START to turn texts back on.
  • Plate texts over iMessage through Blooio. You need a phone that can use iMessage.
  • Your phone number is not shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Blooio and the other services listed under Privacy get what they need to do their job.
  • Plate is an early test. It can get things wrong. Check anything important yourself, like a deadline.
  • Example message: "Next: Essay outline, 30 min. Aim to be done by 4:00 PM."
