Plate

Text it what's on your mind. It handles your calendar.

Early test at Creighton University. Plate is a small project by a Creighton student. It is being tried with a small group of friends before anything wider.

What it does

  1. You text Plate a screenshot, a photo of a flyer, or a message with a date in it.
  2. Plate reads it and works out the event: what, when and where.
  3. It adds the event to your Google Calendar, or asks you one short question if it is unsure.
  4. It tells you what it did. You can say "undo" to remove it.

Privacy

  • What Plate keeps: your phone number, the events it adds for you, and the text of your messages.
  • Screenshots and photos: read once and deleted right after.
  • Your calendar: Plate only adds and removes events that it created. It never deletes your own events.
  • Sharing: Plate does not sell your information and does not share it with anyone for marketing. Message text is processed by an AI service (Anthropic) and a texting service (Twilio) only to do the job you asked for.
  • Delete your data: text DELETE or email the address below and it will be removed.

Text message terms

  • You only get texts from Plate after you text Plate first, or after you opt in by texting the number.
  • Plate replies to your messages and sends confirmations about your calendar. Message frequency depends on how much you use it.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Your phone number is not shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
